  On 2026-11-08: 2 follow-up(s) queued (Q-2026-001, Q-2026-002). Elena already accepted, so she is skipped.
  On 2026-11-12: 0 follow-up(s). The rule is one reminder, then stop.

Step 6 — Invoice reminders stop when payment is recorded
  On 2026-11-17 (3 days before due): INV-2026-001 Upcoming

  INV-2026-001 paid=True, last reminder=Upcoming.

Done. PDFs are in out/quotes and out/invoices; approved mail is in out/outbox; history is in out/state.json.
